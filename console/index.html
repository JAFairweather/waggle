waggle console

Who is admitted to your community, who may task your agents, and by whose signature. This page reads the signed grants off the public relays and verifies every signature here, in your browser. It needs no access to your bridge host and no key of any kind — an admission is a public signed event, so seeing the truth of it requires no privilege.

Why the scope column may say “opaque.” A grant binds to its subject as a salted hash, so a passer-by cannot tell which channel — or which agent — a grant is for, and two grants into the same place cannot be linked. The salt rides publicly in the tag, so anyone who already knows the subject can recompute the hash and confirm the match. Enter the subject above and matching grants resolve; leave it blank and they stay opaque, which is the privacy property working rather than a failure to load.
Read-only, and deliberately. Issuing or revoking is a signing act and belongs where the key lives — tools/grant.mjs and tools/grant-setup.sh. A console that could revoke would need a key in the browser, which is a larger surface than the convenience is worth. What is missing is never invented: if a relay refuses or fails, this says so rather than quietly showing you a shorter list and letting you read it as “nobody is admitted.”
