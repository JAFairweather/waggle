Manage following

Owners can add or remove one public writer. Your signer publishes a narrow, bridge-addressed command; the bridge verifies that you are an approver, persists the change, and begins the consent invitation. This page never receives bridge-host access.

Consent states. Pending means the author has not yet been asked. Asked means Waggle delivered one consent invitation. Active means the author signed a current consent for this hive. Revoked means the author withdrew it; no new posts cross. If the state is older than fifteen minutes, the page says so rather than presenting a stale list as live.
